By Aa'id Abdullah al Qarni

In relation to fasting, this book contains the most pertinent Qur'anic verses, authentic ahadith, delightful poetry and touching advice.

It is therefore, a book for the righteous when they meet for pleasant conversations. It is also a gift for wayfarers when they break their journeys for rest, a treasure for those who share mutual love and respect

For Ramadan is indeed the noblest month and its days are the sweetest days.
