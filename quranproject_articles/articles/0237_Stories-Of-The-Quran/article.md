## Content

The Glorious Qur'an will continue to be the sole everlasting miracle till the Day of Judgement.
It is the Word of Allah, Exalted be He, sent to His chosen Prophet, Muhammad (pbuh) who endeavoured with all his might and main to convey and explain it to the whole world.
