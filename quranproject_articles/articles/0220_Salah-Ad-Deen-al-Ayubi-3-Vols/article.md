## Content

The book Salah ad-Deen Al-Ayubi (Salahuddin Al Ayyubi) is a fascinating 3-volume biography of a man who looms larger than life throughout history, yet who remains largely unknown to people outside the Arab world.
Dr. Sallabi's meticulous research has effectively filled that gap in our knowledge. His work is more than just biography, as the reader will see.
