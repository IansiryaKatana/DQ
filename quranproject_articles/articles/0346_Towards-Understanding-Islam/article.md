## Content

This introduction to Islam by the eminent scholar Mawdudi, reflects his trademark rational, practical, and simple style of presentation. Leaving extended discussions of rituals to others, this work for the Muslim and non-Muslim alike focuses on what Muslims believe and why, devoting attention to the concept of God, Prophethood in Islam and the practical nature of the Islamic law.
