This commentary is a compilation gathered from a number of commentaries written. These being: Tafseer at-Tabaree Tafseer al-Qurtubi [Jaami lil Ahkaam al-Qur'an] Tafseer ibn Katheer, Tafseer ash-Shawkanee [Fath al-Qadeer] Tafseer as-Sa`dee [Tafseer al-Kareem ar-Rahman] Tafseer ash-Shanqeetee [Adwaa al-Bayaan fee Eedaah al-Qur'an bil Qur'an].
