## Content

Riyadh al-Saaliheen or The Meadows of the Righteous, also referred to as The Gardens of the
Righteous
(
Arabic
: رياض الصالحين
Riyadh as-Salihin
or
Riyadh
as-Saaliheen), is a compilation of verses from the Qur'an supplemented by hadith narratives written by Al-Nawawi from Damascus (1233-1277).
