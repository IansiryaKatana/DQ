## Content

Dr Muhammad Ismail (Author). In this book, the author mentions the virtues of Hijab. The characteristics of the Hijab are discussed, bringing glad tidings promised (by Allah) to those women adhering to it. He also points out the danger of dazzling displays of ornaments. Therafter, a Japanese woman's acceptance of Islam and her view of the Hijab is included.
