## Content

Nothing is more important to the believer than entrance into Paradise and the avoidance of Hell. This will be the fruit of his/her deeds in this life. This topics from the topics of the unseen is a major issue of faith.The author has indeed covered all the issues of this reality in this book. The description of Paradise and Hell and its people will make every reader more conscious of his ultimate goal in life and keep him/her busy in the remembrance of its realities.
