## Content

Imam Suyuti has listed 55 names of the Qur'an given by the Qur'an itself. Amongst them are:
Al-Qur'an [that which is recited]
Al-Kitab [written down]
Kareem [most noble]
KalamAllah [speech of Allah]
Nur [ light in amidst a world of darkness]
Huda [guidance away from the path of misguidance]
Rahmah [mercy from Him]
Al-Furqan [Criterion to distinguish right from wrong]
Shifa [cure for the diseases of the Heart like doubt, hypocrisy, etc]
Dhikr [the best way of Reminding oneself about Allah, Akhirah, Haqq]
Mubarak [blessed]
Hakeem [full of Wisdom]
Hablillah [Rope of Allah - without which you will drown]
Sirat al-Mustaqeem [It is the Straight Path]
Ahsan al-Hadeeth [Best of speech]
Ruh [it is the source of life to the heart [spiritually]
AmrAllah [from the commands of Allah]
Majeed [honoured]
Basheer [gives good news]
Nadheer [warns of bad events to happen to those who turn away]
